What is a promo period for new models?

Models

As a new model, you receive a special New Model Promotion to help you start your streaming career on our platform.
The New Model Promotion now lasts up to 30 days and includes a total of 80 streaming hours. The promotion ends automatically when you either reach the 80-hour limit or the 30-day period expires.

During this period, you receive:

  • PA “NEW” badge on your stream thumbnail
  • Placement in the “New Models” category
  • These perks improve your visibility and help you reach more potential viewers. Make sure to use all 80 streaming hours before the 30 days are up to get the most out of your promotion.

When does the New Model Promotion start?

Your promotion starts after your first successful broadcast. Once your profile and streaming tools (camera, microphone, etc.) are set up, you’ll have 15 free minutes at the beginning of your first broadcast to finalize everything.

After streaming for at least 15 minutes, you’ll automatically receive an initial StripScore of 125 points, which marks the official start of your 30-day promo period.

How does the extension work for already active new models?

If you’re a new model whose promo period has already started before the update:

  • Your total New Model Promotion is now extended to 30 days.
  • Your total promotional streaming time increases to 80 hours.
  • Any hours you’ve already streamed are deducted from the new total.

Why did I receive fewer tokens in my payout?

Payouts include tokens earned between Monday 12:00 AM and Sunday 11:59 PM (UTC). Any tokens earned after Sunday midnight are rolled into the next week’s payout. So don’t worry, your tokens aren’t lost. Any new earnings will simply show up in your next payout.
